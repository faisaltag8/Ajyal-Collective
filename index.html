Who We Are

Research that serves
investors, not bankers.

Ajyal Collective brings together a diverse group of some of the top minds in leading universities and professional organizations across the world to provide high-quality equity research with unique insights from real industry experts.

We are a not-for-profit whose main focus is centered around our passion for developing a deep understanding of industries and companies — to provide actionable and valuable insights to the masses, in a straightforward, easy-to-understand way.

The Problem

Investment banks and traditional research institutions are biased in their recommendations — the companies they cover are often also their investment banking clients.

The Gap

Retail investors are often intimidated by financial markets and struggle to navigate the complexities around valuing and investing in public equities.

Our Difference

We provide truly independent, conflict-free research — no banking clients, no hidden agendas. Just rigorous analysis made accessible to everyone.
